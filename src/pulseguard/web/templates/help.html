How PulseGuard works, in short answers.

What is a kind?

Each monitor checks one thing: http, keyword, defacement, tcp, dns, tls or heartbeat.

Why 3 failures?

One failed check can be a blip. Three in a row opens an incident, so you are not woken for noise.

What is a heartbeat?

A job calls /ping/<token> when it runs. If it goes silent past the grace time, PulseGuard opens an incident. Use pulseguard push for an agent.

What does defacement do?

It learns a SHA-256 of the page body and alerts when it changes. Use Learn baseline after an intended edit. You can also learn from the first successful check.

Why is there a grey gap?

PulseGuard has no data for that slice. A gap never counts as downtime. Grey means no data.

Is the evidence pack legal advice?

No. It is not legal advice. It is a timestamped record of what PulseGuard saw.
